Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This page explains what information TODAY NEWSPAPER (todaynewspaperng.com) collects when you use the website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little as possible and to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Reading articles

When you open an article, your browser creates a random reading identifier (for example "3f2a9c...") and keeps it on your device. This identifier, together with the article you opened, is sent to our server so that each device is counted only once per article in our view counts. It does not contain your name, email address or phone number.

You can remove this identifier at any time by clearing your browser's site data for todaynewspaperng.com.

Preferences stored on your device

We use your browser's local storage to remember simple settings, such as dark mode, whether you have already seen our notices, and some display choices. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Ideas and votes

If you submit an idea, we store the information you provide (title, location, category, your reason and, unless you choose to stay anonymous, your name). When you vote on an idea, we record a device identifier and your IP address to prevent repeated votes.

Donations and reminders (Open Hearts Foundation)

When you make a donation, we collect your name, email address, phone number and the amount you give. Payments are processed by our payment provider, Flutterwave, which receives the details needed to complete the payment under its own privacy policy. We do not store your card details.

If you choose to receive donation reminders, your browser gives us a notification subscription so we can send you reminders at the frequency you selected. You can turn notifications off at any time in your browser settings.

How we use and share information

We use this information only to run the website: counting views, managing ideas and votes, processing donations and sending the reminders you asked for. We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the site (such as our hosting, database and payment providers), or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purposes above, or as required by law (for example, records of donations).

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to its use. To do so, contact us at infos@todaynewspaperng.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
